Privacy Policy: Buttercup Bakery

Effective date: 17 November 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Buttercup Bakery (the "app") handles information. It covers the app on Android (Google Play, package com.veldenix.buttercupbakery) and on iPhone and iPad (App Store). The app is made by Veldenix ("we", "us").

The short version

  • We don't ask for, collect or store any personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no in-app purchases and no servers of our own.
  • The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect certain device information to show those ads and to measure them, as described below.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your choice before personalised ads are used. You can change that choice at any time.
  • On iPhone and iPad, your device's advertising identifier is used only if you allow this app to track you.

Information the app keeps on your device

The app saves your progress and settings only on your device:

  • which orders you have packed, and today's special
  • the pastries you have discovered (your Recipe Book)
  • your hints and ribbons
  • whether sound effects are on
  • when the last ads were shown, so that ads stay infrequent

We never receive this information. If you back up your device (Android's device backup, or iCloud or computer backup on iPhone and iPad), the backup may include it, under Google's or Apple's terms.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show:

  • an optional video you can choose to watch for an extra hint
  • an occasional full-screen ad between completed orders (never while a puzzle is being played)

To deliver ads, limit how often they appear, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process the following from your device:

  • IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location
  • Device and advertising identifiers on Android: the advertising ID and the app set ID
  • Device and advertising identifiers on iPhone and iPad: the identifier for vendors (IDFV) and, only if you have allowed this app to track you, the advertising identifier (IDFA)
  • App interactions and diagnostics: for example app launches, ad views and taps, and app performance information

On recent Android versions, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may also use Android's Privacy Sandbox advertising features (ad topics and ad attribution), which you control in your device's settings. On iPhone and iPad, ad measurement may use Apple's privacy-preserving ad attribution (SKAdNetwork).

Google may share this information with its advertising partners as described in its own policies. Data is sent encrypted (TLS).

Google's policies apply to this processing:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

We don't receive your personal information from Google. We only see combined, anonymous statistics about ad performance.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent to personalised ads. To change your choice later, open the game's menu (the pause button) and tap Privacy choices.
  • Tracking on iPhone and iPad: the advertising identifier (IDFA) is available to the app only if you allow tracking. You can change this at any time in Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking. Declining doesn't limit the game in any way.
  • Advertising ID on Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (on most devices: Settings, then Privacy, then Ads, or Settings, then Google, then Ads).
  • Google's controls: https://adssettings.google.com and https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy.
  • Playing without ads: the game works offline. When the device has no internet connection, no ads are requested.

Children

Buttercup Bakery is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app itself collects none from anyone. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we'll help.

Permissions

On Android, the app requests only the permissions that the Google Mobile Ads SDK requires:

  • internet access and network state, to load ads
  • the advertising ID, and Android's ad services (ad ID, ad topics and ad attribution)
  • keeping the device awake and running a foreground service, which libraries inside the Google Mobile Ads SDK declare for short background tasks

On iPhone and iPad, the only permission the app may ask for is Apple's permission to track (App Tracking Transparency), described above.

On both, the app doesn't access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location services or files.

Data deletion

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. To clear your progress, open the game's menu and tap Reset all progress. Your privacy choices and the ad-timing record are kept so that ads stay infrequent. To remove everything the app keeps on your device, uninstall it (on Android you can also clear its storage in the device settings). For data held by Google, use the Google controls listed above.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles information (for example, by adding a new service), we'll update this page and its effective date before that version is released.
